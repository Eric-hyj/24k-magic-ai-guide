# 24K Magic AI — Product Guide

**Website: [24K Magic AI](https://24kmagic-ai.com/)**

**Status: preview — not accepting purchases yet.** The complete generation workflow and real output quality have not yet been validated. This repository documents the intended product; it does not claim that paid generation is currently available.

## What is being built?

A focused website for people who want a 24K Magic inspired two-person video using a photo of themselves and a photo of a friend or partner.

The planned delivery is one complete **30-second, 720p, 16:9 video** featuring two people. Users should use their own photos or photos they have permission to use. Identity consistency, motion quality, and the transition between video sections still need real generation testing.

## Intended workflow

1. Review the example style and the photo requirements.
2. Upload two clear photos, one for each person, with their permission.
3. Choose a one-time video pack when purchases become available.
4. Sign in or register to keep access to the purchase and generated videos. The intended checkout also supports paying before registration.
5. Generate the video, check its status, and download the finished result.

These steps describe the intended experience. Payment acceptance and real generation are not yet open in this preview.
