# 24K Magic AI — Product Guide

**Website: [24K Magic AI](https://24kmagic-ai.com/)**

24K Magic AI is a browser-based video tool for people who want to put themselves and a friend in a cruise dance remake. One-time purchases and video generation are now open.

## What you give it, and what you get

Use two clear photos of consenting adults. Photo 1 supplies the dancer; photo 2 supplies the friend reacting in the corridor and final comic scene. The output format is one complete, approximately **30-second, 720p, 16:9 MP4** with the cruise dance soundtrack. Seedance 2.0 Mini creates the sequence in two parts, combined into one downloadable file.

The homepage example is labeled as an original trend reference, separate from videos generated through this website. AI results can vary in facial detail, motion and continuity.

## How to use it

1. Choose the number of videos you want and pay through Waffo.
2. Sign in with Google or email and password. You can pay before registration; return in the same browser to link your purchase.
3. Select a clear photo for each role and confirm both people consent.
4. Start generation and check progress in your account.
5. Play the result and download the completed MP4.
